Company Profile

From a bold idea to a mobile food movement.

CRUNCH CARTEL is a South African mobile eatery bringing Mexican-inspired comfort food to neighbourhoods, events, and celebrations with fresh ingredients, bold flavour, and a vibrant street-food experience.

Our story

Built on flavour, culture, and unforgettable service.

Crunch Cartel was created to bring people together through bold, satisfying food that feels familiar yet exciting. Our menu blends Mexican-inspired flavours with local favourites, all served with energy, creativity, and the kind of hospitality that turns a meal into a memorable moment.

At a glance

  • Mobile catering for events and markets
  • Freshly prepared meals made to order
  • Flexible menu options for every crowd
  • Proudly serving communities across South Africa
